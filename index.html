Zeerocodes Legal, Privacy & NDPR Compliance

1. Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) Compliance

Zeerocodes processes user information strictly for account identity, educational delivery, and security audit execution. Code repositories uploaded for VibeScan audits are stored in encrypted temporary buffers, processed asynchronously, and discarded following audit completion.

2. Security Audit Scope & Liability

VibeScan conducts independent manual and automated assessments based on the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications framework. VibeCert™ certification signifies adherence to evaluated standards at the time of issuance and does not constitute an absolute guarantee against emerging zero-day vulnerabilities.

3. Webhook & Payment Integrity

All payments made via Paystack or Flutterwave are verified via server-to-server cryptographic signatures. API secrets are strictly isolated on protected infrastructure.
